## Step 1 — Preconditions

**Follow the shared preconditions reference** at [`../_shared/preconditions.md`](../../../_shared/preconditions.md)
to load the repo profile, verify authentication, and prepare the commit identity shorthand.

The later steps read four profile fields: *Default branch*, *Full test*, *PR title convention* and
*Commit identity*. **`<commit-identity>`** stands for the *Commit identity* author line. Today no
step commits; a commit a step adds goes through `guarded-commit.sh` with that line **before** the
branch name, as the shared reference shows.
